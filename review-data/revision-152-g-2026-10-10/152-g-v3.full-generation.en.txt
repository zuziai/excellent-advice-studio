Create one finished, square 1:1 narrative illustration for a thoughtful Persian book for adult readers. The underlying idea is that correcting a weakness in ourselves helps us more than spending our attention judging another person's shortcomings. Make that idea visible through a simple humane park-jogging incident, without an explanatory caption or moral lecture.

COMPOSITION AND READING ORDER
Three spacious scenes within one square page: two equal upper scenes and one wide lower scene. Read the upper RIGHT first, the upper LEFT second, then the LOWER wide scene. Narrow clean white paper gutters separate the scenes, with no heavy comic borders. Every scene has softly irregular watercolor endings. Preserve generous clean white paper outside the painting on all four file edges. Full bodies, complete feet, shoes, hands, important objects and thought bubbles remain fully inside their scenes.

SETTING
A sunny, welcoming park with a broad pale terracotta jogging path. Beyond the path are a blue lake or river, restrained green shrubbery, trees and a softly distant modern city skyline. A substantial leafy tree gives a natural painted upper-left boundary in the scenes. A simple wooden park bench with dark metal supports is visible in the background of the upper-left scene, and much smaller behind the runners in the lower scene. A slender old-fashioned park lamp stands toward the right in the upper-right and lower scenes. Use the same recognizable path and park across this short sequence of consecutive moments. Keep scenery lived-in but calm; do not add crowds, ornamental clutter, extra activities, banners or trophies.

CHARACTERS
Exactly two simple colorful adult figures, with consistent identity, body color, proportions and minimal facial design in all three scenes:
- YELLOW: the main character, no eyelashes. A clear round yellow head, plain warm-yellow body, modest dark oval eyes, simple eyebrows and mouth. Curious and mildly judgmental first, then surprised at the own loose laces, then relaxed and happy.
- PINK: the other adult jogger, a plain pink body and circular pink head. A few very short fine upper-outer eyelashes are the only main female cue. She has exactly the same neutral adult body structure as yellow, with no bust, narrow waist, pronounced hips or buttocks. She jogs comfortably, with a small calm smile.
Both wear the same general kind of plain cream running shoe with a warm sole and ordinary laces. No clothing, accessories, hair, noses or ears are added to the otherwise plain colored figures.

UPPER RIGHT: ATTENTION ON THE OTHER PERSON'S SHOES
At the beginning of the jogging path, pink jogs a few steps ahead toward the right. Yellow is behind her, beginning a small jogging step, with a mild questioning expression. Yellow's eyes and facial orientation point down and diagonally RIGHT toward PINK'S CREAM RUNNING SHOES, rather than toward yellow's own shoes or merely pink's face. A white cloud-shaped THOUGHT bubble belongs unambiguously to yellow, connected to yellow by a short chain of small thought circles. Its exact text is:
"با این کفشا می‌دوه؟!"
Pink's shoes are ordinary and unchanged in character from the other scenes; the thought expresses yellow's judgment, not an actual exaggerated defect.
Yellow's OWN two shoes have visibly UNTIED, separate shoelaces. Show loose lace ends belonging naturally to each shoe. Do not connect the two shoes with one cord or knot them together. Yellow has not noticed the own laces yet. A small low wooden marker beside the path, entirely inside the scene, carries only:
"شروع"
All legs, feet and shoes are complete and naturally attached. Pink's running torso, neutral pelvis and two bent legs form one anatomically coherent figure.

UPPER LEFT: ATTENTION TURNS TO THE OWN PROBLEM
In the next moment, yellow has paused and sits DIRECTLY ON THE GROUND OF THE SAME JOGGING PATH, not on the nearby bench. The bench remains visibly behind and apart from yellow. Clearly show yellow's neutral hips supported by the path with a soft ground contact shadow. One leg is bent with its knee raised close enough for yellow to reach the shoe; the other leg extends naturally along the ground toward the right. Both legs, both complete cream shoes and their relation to the torso are legible.
Yellow leans forward with a small surprised, slightly embarrassed expression and looks down at the OWN undone laces. Both hands are at the reachable shoe, tying its laces naturally. The head can be slightly tilted through the position of the facial features, but its silhouette remains a true circle. A white cloud-shaped thought bubble, with a trail of small circles clearly belonging to yellow, reads exactly:
"بندهای خودم بازه!"
Pink continues jogging in the background toward the right as a smaller distant figure, with exactly the same identity, neutral body design and cream shoes. Her smaller size comes only from perspective.

LOWER WIDE: BENEFIT OF FIXING THE OWN OBSTACLE
Yellow and pink now jog comfortably toward the right on the same broad path. Pink is ahead and yellow follows with a relaxed happy face. Every shoe has its own neatly tied lace bow. Natural balanced jogging poses, coherent torso-to-pelvis-to-leg anatomy, full feet and clear ground placement. This is an ordinary shared jog, not a competition: no finish line, winning, medal, podium, overtaking drama or reward. Yellow's freer movement is the visible consequence of attending to the own neglected problem. No thought bubble or extra writing in this lower scene.

STRICT CHARACTER DESIGN
Every head is a simple PERFECT CIRCLE, with equal width and height and an uninterrupted circular outline, even in a turned or downward-looking pose. No oval head, chin, jaw, flattened lower arc, hair, ears, nose or neck. The round head joins the upper torso and shoulders directly. A soft darker crescent of the same local body color immediately beneath the head gives a modest head-to-body contact shadow, without becoming a neck segment.
Bodies are simple, balanced MEDIUM ADULT proportions: neither plump/chibi nor spindly or excessively stretched. All genders share the same neutral structure, with almost parallel torso sides and a natural unshaped pelvis. No breasts, tiny pinched waist, waist notch, projected feminine hips, separate rounded buttock bulge or gendered indentations. Limbs connect naturally to the torso and pelvis, especially in the ground-seated shoe-tying pose and all running poses.
Eyes are modest small dark ovals. A tiny light reflection INSIDE an eye is allowed; absolutely no surrounding white sclera, white eyeball with a separate pupil, large doll eyes or frightening blank eyes. Pink's few short fine eyelashes sit on the upper outer edge of these same eyes; adding lashes does not change their shape, size or design. Simple expressive eyebrows and small mouths. No defined pink cheek stickers or sharp circular blush; any cheek warmth is barely perceptible, translucent and diffusely feathered.
Every openly visible hand has THREE FINGERS PLUS ONE THUMB. Hands gripping laces or curled while running may have natural finger occlusion, but must not turn into mittens or acquire extra fingers.

PAINTING STYLE
Authentic moderate hand-painted layered WATERCOLOR on luminous textured white paper. The watercolor is visible INSIDE the heads, torsos, arms, legs and hands as well as the scenery: broad softly irregular overlapping translucent washes, varied pigment density, restrained edge pooling and natural soft wash boundaries. Keep clear contours in darker versions of each figure's OWN body color: dark golden yellow for yellow, deeper warm pink for pink, never black body outlines. Face details and Persian letters may be dark. Warm bright colors, restrained soft shading, believable natural scene shadows, and a kind, gently witty adult-book mood.
Do not replace watercolor with flat/vector fills, marker coloring, uniform fine colored-pencil grain, glossy plastic/3D rendering, repeated embossed texture, heavy mottling, splashes or excessive watercolor staining. No photorealism.

TEXT
The ONLY in-image words are the three exact short Persian texts quoted above, with accurate connected lettering and correct right-to-left direction. Natural colloquial Persian, legible placement, no extra letters, English, captions, numbers, watermark or artist signature. The thought bubbles belong to yellow. The painting must communicate the action and change without an outside explanation.
