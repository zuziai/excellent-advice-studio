Create one finished square 1:1 narrative watercolor illustration for an adult and teenage visual book. Arrange THREE full-width horizontal scenes stacked from top to bottom with thin white gutters and no heavy comic frames. Tell a simple causal story in a warm sewing workshop: a BLUE adult tailor initially thinks of income and impatiently blames a YELLOW adult coworker for tangled thread; blue then experiences the same difficulty and receives yellow's patient help; later blue treats yellow with the patience learned during the work. The change must be visible in treatment of the person, not merely in improved sewing technique. Use only these two consistent characters in every scene. The emotional tone is gentle and humane, with quiet wit in the demanding tailor learning patience from the coworker.

HAND-PAINTED BOOK STYLE
Use real moderate watercolor on textured white paper. Broad translucent overlapping washes and gentle variations of pigment are visible throughout the HEADS, TORSOS, ARMS, LEGS and HANDS, not only at the background edges. Preserve luminous lighter areas with paper showing through and a little soft pigment pooling at natural painted edges. BLUE is shaded with transparent cyan and blue washes, with darker blue contours; YELLOW uses warm lemon and golden washes, with darker ocher contours. Never outline the bodies with black. Use small dark facial marks, soft local-color contact shadows beneath the heads, and quiet floor and chair shadows. The room uses warm peach and cream, light wood, a restrained multicolored rug and soft green plants. No flat vector fills, marker coloring, colored-pencil grain, glossy plastic, heavily mottled paint or airbrushed 3D shading. Blush is omitted or an extremely faint blended watercolor wash, never a sharp circular pink cheek. Natural organic outer paint edges remain inside generous white paper margins. Keep all three rows fully framed, with every seated lower leg, foot and important table support inside its own scene, never clipped at the gutter.

CHARACTER STRUCTURE AND CONTINUITY
Blue and yellow are neutral, medium, balanced ADULT figures with simple almost straight-sided torsos, modest arms and naturally connected seated hips, knees, lower legs and rounded feet. Neither is very thin, chunky, baby-like or chibi. Yellow is a junior adult coworker, not a child. Both genders use the SAME neutral body shape, with no breasts, pinched waist, protruding hips or buttocks, or feminine bulges and indentations. Both wear matching plain straight CREAM aprons with simple shoulder straps and a small rectangular front pocket, and no other clothes. Heads are PERFECT CIRCLES, equal width and height, with no elongated oval, pointed chin, jaw, ears, nose, hair or separate neck. The round head joins the shoulders directly with a soft darker same-color crescent shadow, keeping its boundary clear. Use the same small dark oval eyes on both characters. Tiny white glints INSIDE the dark eyes are allowed; white sclera around them is forbidden. If yellow has a few fine SHORT upper-outer eyelashes, attach them to the existing small eye shape without widening, whitening or restyling the eye. Simple eyebrows and mouth convey discouragement, frustration and later kindness. Closed happy arc eyes are suitable for yellow in the last scene. Each hand has THREE fingers PLUS ONE thumb, with natural grips and genuine occlusion when handling fine thread. Hands, arms and seated lower bodies connect believably. Keep blue consistently on the LEFT and yellow on the RIGHT in all scenes.
