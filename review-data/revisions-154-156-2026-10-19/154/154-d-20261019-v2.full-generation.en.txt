Use case: illustration-story. Create a square hand-painted watercolor two-panel comic for an adult Persian visual life-advice book. Read the panels RIGHT TO LEFT, with the question at right and the quietly witty realization at left. Meaning: discovering your own purpose is itself your mission; the treasure to discover is yourself, rather than money or a chest of rewards.

SETTING AND LAYOUT: Two related side-by-side courtyard scenes, thin clean white gutter, no heavy panel borders. Warm pale plaster walls and a few leafy green plants, a quiet blue door, gentle sunshine, pale blue sky. Keep the people, short dialogue and map the focus. The watercolor scene ends in irregular natural brush edges before a generous clean white outer margin. Everything important, including feet and the map, is fully inside the frame.

CHARACTERS: One cool BLUE adult seeker and one fresh GREEN adult friend, identical in size, color, proportions and identity between panels. Blue has a small practical burgundy backpack. Otherwise both have the same simple neutral adult colored body as the established book figures, no ordinary clothing, no sexed anatomy, no hair. Moderate, balanced limbs and torsos, neither chubby/chibi nor extremely thin. Heads are plain true circles, equal width and height, without jaws, chins or flattened lower arcs. Draw a soft shadow in the local body color beneath each head to distinguish the head from the torso. Outlines are darker shades of their own body colors, deep blue for blue and deep green for green, never black outlines around bodies. Simple eyebrows, expressive small mouth, small dark vertical oval eyes with tiny light-reflection glints, absolutely no white sclera or human eyeballs. No need for eyelashes in this scene. Each visible full hand has exactly three fingers plus one thumb, attached naturally to its single arm; exactly two arms and two hands per figure.

RIGHT PANEL, QUESTION: Blue stands on the right, puzzled, looking at green. Blue touches the chin with one hand and holds a small closed brown spiral personal notebook against the chest with the other. The notebook cover is plain, without letters. The friend stands on the left, listening kindly, hands naturally behind the back, not a teacher or official. One white speech bubble with tail to BLUE says exactly in Persian: "مأموریت اصلی‌م کی شروع می‌شه؟" No other text in this panel.

LEFT PANEL, REALIZATION: Same two people: green at left and blue at right. Both look down together at a large single unfolded tan parchment TREASURE MAP held by GREEN. Green supports the lower edge with one hand and points to its destination with the other. Blue leans in slightly with a dawn of recognition, warm small smile and hands gently clasped near the chest. The green pointing fingertip is close to the blue-self destination on the actual paper. The sheet is an unfolded map, NOT a spiral notebook, poster or three pictures. Give it modest irregular parchment edges and sparse painted mountain, tree and river marks. A simple dotted red route winds across the paper and ends at ONE small unmistakable BLUE HUMANOID SYMBOL, matching the seeker: a circular blue head, simple neutral blue torso and limbs, tiny simple smiling face. This is a flat painted pictogram on the paper, not a miniature living person. The route's treasure is the seeker himself; NO chest, gold, gems, trophy, money or other prize. A few small warm radiating strokes emphasize that blue-self destination, without adding text. Keep the map naturally angled toward its TWO READERS: the blue pictogram's head is toward the nearer/lower edge of the paper and its feet toward the farther/upper edge, hence upside-down to the external viewer. Do not turn the map's icon upright to the external viewer at the cost of its correct orientation to the characters. The map must remain large and clear, not crammed with decorative marks. One white speech bubble with its tail correctly attached to GREEN says exactly: "همین پیدا کردن راه خودت!" No caption, labels or other words on the map.

MEDIUM: Luminous but moderate REAL hand-painted watercolor, translucent overlapping wash layers visible inside bodies as well as scenery. Notice textured paper, subtle pigment pooling, soft natural watercolor blooms and varying wash density. Keep soft local-color volume and a few restrained highlights without making plastic or shiny 3D. No marker, colored-pencil hatching, vector flats, dense digital noise or thick black figure contours. Warm, kind, memorable human humor, family friendly, suitable for adult readers and readily understood without an explanatory caption. Only the two specified short Persian speech texts, legible correct right-to-left lettering. No English, watermark, logo, panel numbers, extra panels, added people or props.
